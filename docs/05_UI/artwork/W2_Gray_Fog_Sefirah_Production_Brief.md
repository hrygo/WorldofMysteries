# W2 — Gray Fog / Sefirah Production Brief

> Program: Premium Art A1  
> Artwork ID: `W2_GRAY_FOG_SEFIRAH`  
> Status: PRODUCTION BRIEF LOCKED  
> Role: `scene`

## 1. Product purpose

W2 defines the high-order liminal visual domain used by Fate / Worldline / Sefirah-adjacent presentation. It must feel radically different from ordinary city reality while remaining restrained enough to support real macOS UI.

The goal is not “fantasy castle in clouds.” The image should communicate impossible scale, obscured causality, high-order observation and distance from ordinary physical space.

## 2. Canon-safe visual thesis

**Gray Fog is a semantic high-order domain, not a universal visual filter.**

W2 may use:
- vast layered gray fog;
- depth that does not obey ordinary perspective;
- hints of ancient stone, dark metal, brass or worn monumental structure;
- subtle table/dais/threshold geometry if useful to imply observation and intervention;
- distant suspended points / stars / threads / apertures as restrained symbolic evidence;
- impossible horizon or vertical depth.

The scene must remain interpretive and product-composable rather than asserting unsupported literal architectural detail.

## 3. Forbidden visual shortcuts

- no generic fairy-tale castle in clouds;
- no all-purple cosmic nebula;
- no sci-fi hologram grid;
- no neon constellation UI;
- no giant character silhouette seated on a throne as the entire composition;
- no omnipresent runes or fake readable inscriptions;
- no tentacle mass;
- no giant magical portal dominating every crop;
- no baked labels / interface text / glyph walls;
- no copied official animation/game/comic composition.

## 4. Visual verb

- **Primary verb:** `observe`
- **Secondary verb:** `separate`

The viewer should feel positioned outside ordinary causality: able to observe distant threads of reality while still separated from them by scale and fog.

## 5. Material & atmosphere direction

### Physical anchors
Use only a few materially convincing anchors so the space does not become generic vapor:
- weathered stone edge / platform / table-like plane;
- aged brass or darkened metal detail;
- blackened structural fragments partly disappearing into fog;
- cold old-silver highlights.

### Atmosphere
- layered neutral gray fog with meaningful depth;
- subtle light shafts with no obvious physical source;
- low-saturation silver-blue highlights;
- occasional warm brass remnant to connect with the app’s material language.

### Supernatural abnormality
- perspective layers can disagree subtly;
- distant points may sit at impossible depth;
- fog can form discontinuous vertical volumes;
- faint causal threads may appear and vanish without reading as a sci-fi HUD.

## 6. Composition contract

### Master / derivatives
- Master target: `4096×2560`
- Runtime target: `2560×1600`
- Optional header crop: `2400×900`

### Focus zone
Primary high-order focal structure: **center-right**, approximately x=50–80%, y=30–72%.

Possible focal construction:
- partial ancient platform / edge suspended in fog;
- a controlled cluster of distant silver points / causal threads;
- one impossible depth break beyond the platform.

### Quiet zone
Reserve **left 34–40%** as low-detail gray tonal space suitable for Fate title/status copy and overlays.

The quiet zone must not contain:
- a bright portal;
- star cluster directly under text;
- dense symbolic pattern;
- hard architectural edge crossing expected headline area.

### Crop reserve
- main focal anchor >=12% from image edges;
- no unique semantic subject in outer 8%;
- 16:10 crop preserves fog depth + focal anchor;
- 2400×900 crop preserves horizon/depth cue without looking like generic cloud photography.

## 7. Contrast / UI overlay contract

Approved runtime treatment:
- localized left-side charcoal/gray scrim for text;
- optional WOM surface for dense facts;
- decorative fog may be visually reduced in Increased Contrast mode.

Acceptance:
- `textPrimary` on approved quiet-zone scrim >=4.5:1;
- important long title/status copy target >=7:1;
- causal-thread detail must never compete with readable UI text.

## 8. Color target

Dominant:
- neutral gray;
- blue-gray;
- charcoal;
- old silver.

Secondary:
- very restrained aged brass;
- pale cold light;
- near-black structural silhouettes.

Forbidden dominant:
- saturated violet;
- cyan laser blue;
- gold fantasy heaven-light;
- bright RGB accents.

## 9. Generation / production prompt core

Create an original premium cinematic high-order gray-fog scene for a macOS dark-fantasy application, expressing impossible scale, observation and separation from ordinary causality. Use vast layered neutral gray and blue-gray fog with convincing volumetric depth, subtle old-silver light, a few materially real anchors such as weathered stone, blackened metal and restrained aged-brass details, and one center-right ancient platform or threshold fragment fading into impossible depth. Distant silver points or faint causal threads may appear, but they must feel metaphysical rather than like a sci-fi hologram. Preserve a calm low-detail darker quiet zone across the left third for SwiftUI titles and status. The image should feel austere, ancient and spatially impossible, not like a fantasy castle in clouds. No typography, no UI, no logos, no giant runes, no neon constellations, no purple nebula, no floating castle, no tentacles, no excessive bloom. Composition must remain coherent at 16:10 desktop crop and wide 2400×900 header crop.

## 10. Negative prompt / rejection cues

Reject if:
- it reads as “castle in clouds”;
- a throne/character becomes the main poster subject;
- fog is just a photographic cloud bank with no high-order depth;
- visual language looks like sci-fi HUD / hologram;
- purple/cyan becomes dominant;
- impossible glyph text appears;
- no stable quiet zone remains;
- architectural detail becomes more important than spatial mystery;
- the scene could be mistaken for W1 city atmosphere with extra fog.
