## Visual design for the first candidate

Produce one square, text-free premium game illustration of the silver mirror itself. Use an antique silver frame, restrained non-letter ornament and two eye-like black cabochon side decorations as a provisional design choice pending full Canon review. The complete object occupies roughly 65–75% of the frame and retains generous crop reserve. Choose an intimate three-quarter view, not a cathedral interior or a generic pocket watch.

Visual verb: **observe / answer**. A subtly impossible reflection and two watchful side ornaments convey perception and the discomfort of being watched in return. A deep smoky green/silver reflection may be used as artistic interpretation, not as a claimed mandatory Canon color. Do not put questions, answers, captions, numbers, pseudo-writing or a face in the glass.

Material hierarchy: tarnished old silver, polished worn edges, dark cabochons, a clear glass surface with unnatural optical depth. Keep readable midtones and soft cool fill; limited warm reflected light adds depth without turning the object gold. A simple desaturated dark-teal textile supports the object. Any rear support belongs to staging, not the Artifact's asserted body.

Exclude crimson moons, city skylines, gothic towers, grand halls, astrolabes, chains of suspended worlds, extra artifacts, people, borders, logos, labels and UI. Avoid purple glow, tentacle decoration, gold loot-rarity styling and photographic product-ad rendering. The earlier scene palette is a world-level reference, not a template to stamp on this object.
